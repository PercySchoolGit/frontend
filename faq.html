Student Center FAQs

What are the operating hours of the Student Center?

The Student Center is open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and weekends from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

How can students reserve a study or meeting room?

Students can reserve rooms online using the Room Booking page on this website by selecting an available room and filling out the form.

Is high-speed Wi-Fi available throughout the whole building?

Yes, secure wireless network access (eduroam / UH Wireless) is available for all enrolled students and faculty members.

Where can I get food or coffee inside the facility?

There are multiple dining options on the ground floor, including a coffee shop, grab-and-go snack bars, and rotating food trucks outside.

Who do I contact if I lose something here?

Lost and found services are managed at the main front security and information desk on the first floor.
